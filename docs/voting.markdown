## Checking Voter Status (MS)
1. Go to sos.ms.gov/yall-vote 
2. Scroll down and click on "Voter Registration Verification"  
        (or scan QR code below)
3. Fill in your County, Last and First name, last four digits of you SSN, and your birthday. 
4. Click the "I'm not a robot" box. Pass the CAPTCHA test.
5. Click "Search".
6. Select the name/address combination that matches you. (Make it’s circle filled in.)
7. Click “Verify”
8. Hopefully, it will show “You are a registered Voter”. If not, then you need to contact the Circuit Clerk’s office. See www.sos.ms.gov/elections-voting/county-election-information for more information.

## Registering to Vote
- Note: Do this process on a desktop or laptop if you don’t know how to print from your phone.

1. Go to sos.ms.gov/yall-vote 
2. Scroll down and click on "Voter Information & Registration" (or scan QR code below)
3. Click on “Voter Registration Application”
4. Print the form
5. Read the instructions and fill out appropriately, including MS Driver’s License # (or last 4 of SSN if no Driver’s License)
6. Package completed form in an envelope and send or hand-deliver to the relevant County Circuit Clerk listed on second page.
